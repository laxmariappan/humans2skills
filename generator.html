Tips for a Great Skill

  • Be specific. "Always patient" is good. "Explained things three different ways until it clicked" is better.
  • Focus on feeling. How did they make you feel? Capable? Calm? Understood?
  • Think of moments. A specific time they helped you can capture their energy better than general traits.
  • It's okay to idealize. You're capturing the version of them that helped you. That's valid.
