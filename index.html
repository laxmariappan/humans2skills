What's This About?

We all have people who changed how we work, think, or show up. A colleague who made debugging feel less lonely. A manager who believed in you before you believed in yourself. A mentor who explained things in a way that finally clicked.

These people shaped us. Their energy stays with us long after we've moved on.

Humans 2 Skills is a way to capture that energy. To honor the people who helped you by turning their approach into something you can call on anytime - through AI.

It's not about replacing them. It's about remembering what they gave you.
