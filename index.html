What's This?

You know that colleague who always knows what to say when you're stuck? Or that friend who makes hard things feel doable?

Their vibe stays with you. Even when they're not around.

Humans 2 Skills lets you capture that vibe and turn it into an AI skill. So next time you need their energy, you can bring it into the conversation.

Like having your favorite people in your pocket.

Tips for Capturing Vibes

  • Be specific. "Patient" is fine. "Explained things three different ways until it clicked" is better.
  • Focus on feeling. How do they make you feel? Capable? Calm? Heard?
  • Think of moments. A specific time they helped you captures their vibe better than general traits.
  • It's your version of them. You're capturing how they show up for you. That's what matters.
